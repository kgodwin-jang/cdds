WHO WE ARE

Vision · Mission · Values

Vision

To be the leading centre of academic and moral formation in northern Ghana, nurturing responsible leaders.

Mission

To educate the whole child through disciplined scholarship, practical skills and community service.

Values

Integrity, community service, resilience and respect for our heritage and environment.
